{% extends "page_view.html" %} {% block page.content %} Privacy policies, terms, user agreements and notices, written and published from the database, with a record of who accepted which version and when. A legal document gets reworded on advice, and the day it changes you need two things at once: the new text in front of people, and proof of what the old text said to everyone who agreed to it. So the documents live in the database. They are written in Markdown, published as ordinary pages on the site, and a published version never changes again. A correction is a new version, and the record of an agreement points at the exact version a person saw.
