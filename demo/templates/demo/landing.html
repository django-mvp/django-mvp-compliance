django-mvp-compliance

Legal documents, consent records and cookie consent. This page is where they get looked at while they are being built.

Writing and publishing a document

The authoring surface is the Django admin. Documents and their versions are under Compliance.

A visitor reads the version in force at /legal/<slug>/. A document with nothing published is invisible to a visitor, which is why the list below links only the ones that have something.

  • Privacy policy
  • Terms of use
  • Impressum

Seeded documents

  • Privacy policy — two published versions, one in force and one superseded.
  • Terms of use — one version, in force.
  • Impressum — a notice with one version in force. People read it and are never asked to agree to it.
  • Community guidelines — five published versions over three years, under a long name.
  • Membership terms — forty published versions since 2013.
  • Delivery information — agreed to once, then made a notice.
  • Cookie policy — a draft and nothing published.
  • Acceptable use — no versions at all.

Rebuild them with python manage.py seed_demo.
