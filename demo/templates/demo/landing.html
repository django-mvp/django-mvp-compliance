django-mvp-compliance

Legal documents, consent records and cookie consent. This page is where they get looked at while they are being built.

Writing and publishing a document

The authoring surface is the Django admin. Documents and their versions are under Compliance.

A visitor reads the version in force at /legal/<slug>/. A document with nothing published is invisible to a visitor, which is why the list below links only the ones that have something.

  • Privacy policy
  • Terms of use

Accounts

Sign in as Can do

Seeded documents
